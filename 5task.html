Whether you love adventure, history or simply relaxing, India has something for every traveller. Start your journey with us and discover the incredible beauty of this country.

Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is famous all over the world for its rich flavours, colourful spices and variety. Every region has its own special dishes, from creamy curries in the north to coconut-based meals in the south and spicy street snacks everywhere in between.

5 Famous Indian Dishes

  1. Butter Chicken / Paneer Butter Masala
  2. Biryani
  3. Masala Dosa
  4. Chole Bhature
  5. Pani Puri

Food Categories

  • North Indian
  • South Indian
  • Street Food
  • Desserts

Dish Descriptions

Biryani
A fragrant rice dish cooked with spices, vegetables or meat.
Masala Dosa
A crispy rice crepe filled with spiced potato, served with sambar and chutney.
Pani Puri
Crispy hollow puris filled with tangy spiced water, chutney and potatoes.

Famous Places in India

Taj Mahal, Agra

A white marble monument built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Red Fort, Delhi

A historic Mughal fort made of red sandstone, where the Prime Minister hoists the flag on Independence Day.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

The "Palace of Winds" is known for its pink sandstone front with hundreds of small windows.

Gateway of India, Mumbai

An arch monument on the waterfront of Mumbai, built in the early 20th century.

Kerala Backwaters

A peaceful network of lagoons and canals where you can enjoy houseboat stays.
